AICTE-approved
institute affiliated to
Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
LIET offers undergraduate engineering programs designed to meet
current industry requirements
, with strong emphasis on:
Core engineering fundamentals
Emerging technologies
Practical exposure & internships
Career-oriented learning
Admissions are conducted strictly as per
AICTE, AKTU and Government of India norms
