Teaching & Non-teaching Staff Grievance Form
Staff Support & Grievance Resolution
The Teaching & Non-Teaching Staff Grievance Form
provides a platform for staff members to raise concerns related to workplace, administrative, or institutional matters. It ensures that every grievance is addressed in a fair, transparent, and respectful manner.
Staff members can submit their concerns confidentially through this form. The institution is committed to reviewing each grievance carefully and resolving it promptly. This process helps maintain a healthy and professional working environment. Staff are encouraged to share their concerns to support continuous improvement within the institution.
