Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC)
The
Lloyd Institute of Engineering and Technology (LIET)
is dedicated to fostering a culture of
innovation, creativity, and entrepreneurship
among students and faculty. IIC provides a platform for
student-led initiatives, innovation challenges
, and
startup incubation
, aimed at transforming ideas into viable solutions. The council supports
entrepreneurial development
through
mentorship, training programs
industry collaborations
, helping to create an ecosystem where students can thrive as innovators and entrepreneurs.
