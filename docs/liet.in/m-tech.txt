Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
To develop graduates who have strong theoretical foundations and advanced practical skills
in computer science and engineering, enabling them to design, analyze, and implement solutions to complex computing problems in industry and research.
To enable graduates to pursue research, development, and higher-level academic opportunities
, preparing them for careers in academia, industry R&D, and entrepreneurial ventures.
To promote professional growth through lifelong learning, ethical practice, and leadership
, equipping students to adapt to evolving technologies and contribute positively to society and their profession.
Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
Apply advanced computer science principles and contemporary tools
to analyze, model, design, and implement effective computing solutions for complex problems in industry and research.
Demonstrate proficiency in current and emerging technologies
(such as algorithms, data structures, distributed systems, and software engineering) to develop optimized, secure, and scalable solutions that meet real-world requirements
Exhibit research aptitude and professional competencies
, including communication, teamwork, ethical practice, and lifelong learning, enabling graduates to contribute meaningfully to technological innovation and societal needs.

Syllabus
Computer Science & Engineering Lab
Data Analytics Lab
The Lab provides the platform to prepare the students with the statistical, mathematical reasoning, machine learning, knowledge discovery and visualization skills, The students will have an opportunity to learn principles, tools and techniques to model various real world problems, analyse them, and discover useful information. The lab facilitates high configuration machines with relevant softwares like Jupyter, R, Python, Keras, tensorflow to name a few.
Database Management System and Object Oriented Lab
The Lab is dedicated for Oriented Languages and Database related practicals and projects. High configuration computer systems with relevant softwares like Java, C++, along with frontend and backend softwares like Net Beans, Visual studio, MySQL are installed to design, develop and implement Database related projects and assignments.
Web Technology and Multimedia Lab
The Lab is dedicated to gain hands experience on web technologies. The high configuration machines are equipped with relevant software tools like Eclipse, visual studio, PHP, Web server, XAMPP, WAMP, LAMP, and Net Beans to name a few.
