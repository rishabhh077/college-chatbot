About LIET Connect
Be the Friend Who Opens Doors
Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology CONNECT
is a dynamic
Student Ambassador & Referral Initiative
by Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology, Greater Noida. It invites students across engineering and technology programs to refer school friends, juniors, and relatives for admission at LIET. Whether you are a current student, a prospective applicant, or an alumnus, your referral plays a meaningful role in shaping future careers.
