Research & Development
R&D Appraisal Policy
Empowering Innovation Through Transparent Evaluation and Research Excellence.
Objectives
Encourage and support innovative research activities
Promote collaboration between faculty, students, and external organizations.
Enhance the institute's visibility and reputation through high-quality research outputs.
Contribute to the development of technologies and solutions that address societal challenges.
Introduction
Purpose:
This policy aims to foster a culture of research and innovation among
faculty members, enhance the quality of education, and contribute to the socioeconomic development of the community.
Scope:
This policy applies to all faculty members and research staff at the Lloyd
Institute of Engineering and Technology.
Governance Structure
Research Committee
Establish a Research Committee comprising faculty members from various departments, tasked with overseeing research activities, funding allocation, and policy implementation.
