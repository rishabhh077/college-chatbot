Understanding the Basics of Engineering
Engineering is the application of science and math to design, build, and maintain structures,
machines, systems, and processes. Engineers use their knowledge to solve problems, improve
efficiency, and create innovations that impact society.
Before diving into the specific courses, let’s first understand what engineering is all
about. Engineering is a field that focuses on designing, developing, and maintaining structures,
systems, machines, and technology. It combines science, mathematics, and practical knowledge to
solve problems and improve the world we live in.

B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering

Eligibility Criteria for Engineering Courses
The eligibility criteria for engineering courses generally include the following:
Educational Qualification:
Candidates must have completed 10+2 or its equivalent with a minimum percentage (typically
50-60%) in subjects like Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
Some colleges may also require proficiency in English.
Entrance Exam:
Most engineering colleges require candidates to appear for entrance exams such as JEE Main,
JEE Advanced, or state-level exams.
Some private institutions may conduct their own entrance tests.
Age Limit:
Candidates are usually required to be at least 17 years old on or before the year of
admission. The upper age limit may vary depending on the institution and the specific
entrance exam.
Minimum Marks:
Candidates must meet the minimum marks requirement as specified by the institution or the
entrance exam (usually based on a percentage or rank).
It’s important to check the specific eligibility requirements of the college or university
you're applying to, as they may vary slightly.
