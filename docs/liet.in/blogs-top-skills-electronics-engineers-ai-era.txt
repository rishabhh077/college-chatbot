Embedded systems are the backbone of modern electronics. From smart appliances to industrial automation systems, embedded technology powers almost every electronic device.

Real-time operating systems (RTOS)

Hardware-software integration
Embedded systems engineers are in high demand across industries including automotive, robotics, healthcare devices, and consumer electronics.
