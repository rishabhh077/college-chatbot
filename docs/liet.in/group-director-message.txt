Message from the Group Director
Dear Aspiring Engineers, Dear Students, Dear Parents, and Esteemed Visitors,
When you visit the
Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology
website, you are not just exploring a college, you are standing at the doorway of a
decision that can shape the next four decades of your life
To our
aspiring B.Tech students
: ask yourself one simple question;
Do I want a degree, or do I want to be ready for the world I will step into?
At LIET, we have consciously moved away from rote learning and conventional classroom-only education. The world today demands engineers who can think, adapt, communicate, and deliver. That is why our focus is on
AI-driven learning, cybersecurity readiness, real-world problem solving, and industry-style exposure from the early years
. We want you to graduate not with hesitation, but with confidence and the ability to contribute from Day One.
existing students
: you are already on this journey. Remember, the opportunities around you—labs, live projects, case studies, mentors, and industry exposure—are meaningful only when you choose to engage deeply with them. Use your time here to build not just technical expertise, but also discipline, ethics, resilience, and professional maturity. These will define you far beyond your first job.
parents and visitors
: choosing an institution is ultimately about trust. At Lloyd, we recognise that when you entrust us with a young mind, you entrust us with a future. Our commitment is to nurture your children into
industry-ready professionals and responsible individuals
, equipped with skills, values, and a mindset of lifelong learning.
As an academician and administrator with over two decades in higher education, I firmly believe that
education must evolve as fast as the world outside the campus gates
. At LIET, we continuously challenge ourselves to remain relevant, industry-aligned, and student-focused—without compromising on ethics or values.
I warmly invite you to explore our ecosystem, interact with our faculty and students, and experience what makes LIET truly different. If you are ready to work hard, think differently, and grow consistently,
LIET will walk every step of the journey with you
Warm regards,
Prof. Vandana Arora Sethi
Chief Strategy Officer & Head of Growth
Lloyd Group of Institutions
Alumnus – IIM Bangalore I LLM (University of Buckingham) | Gold Medallist – (University of Delhi)
