Ensuring Quality, Enhancing Excellence
INTERNAL QUALITY ASSURANCE CEL
(IQAC).
The
of Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology (LIET) is established to promote continuous quality improvement in academic, administrative and institutional processes.
IQAC facilitates systematic quality assurance through planning, monitoring, documentation, feedback, academic audits, outcome-based education, stakeholder engagement and continuous improvement. It coordinates quality initiatives across departments and supports institutional preparedness for accreditation and regulatory requirements.

Objectives
of IQAC
The Objective Of IQAC are aligned with the institution’s commitment to quality assurance academic excellence and continuous improvement in all its functions.
To develop and maintain a quality-oriented institutional environment.
To monitor and improve academic and administrative processes.
To promote quality initiatives in teaching and learning.
To facilitate implementation of Outcome-Based Education.
To collect, analyze and act upon stakeholder feedback.
To conduct academic and institutional quality audits.
To document quality initiatives and improvement measures
To identify and promote best practices.
To monitor corrective and preventive actions.
To support NBA, AICTE and other accreditation-related quality processes
Major Functions of IQAC
• Preparation and implementation of the annual IQAC action plan.
• Coordination of academic and administrative quality initiatives.
• Monitoring of teaching-learning
processes.
• Promotion of Outcome-Based Education and attainment analysis.
• Coordination of feedback collection and analysis.
• Review of quality-related institutional
data.
• Documentation and maintenance of quality records.
• Monitoring of corrective and improvement actions.
• Promotion of best practices and innovative initiatives.
• Coordination with departments and institutional committees.
• Support for NBA, AICTE and other statutory/accreditation requirements.
