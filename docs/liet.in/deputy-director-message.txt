About Campus
Deputy Director's Message
Welcome to Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology (LIET), Greater Noida.
The world is witnessing a remarkable transformation driven by technology, innovation, and knowledge. In this rapidly evolving environment, the role of an educational institution extends far beyond imparting academic knowledge. It is about nurturing responsible professionals, inspiring innovation, and preparing future leaders who can create meaningful impact on society.
At LIET, we have created a vibrant academic ecosystem where learning is experiential, research is purposeful, and innovation is encouraged at every stage. Our curriculum is enriched through industry partnerships, project-based learning, internships, research opportunities, and entrepreneurship initiatives that enable students to bridge the gap between classroom concepts and real-world applications.
Our greatest strength lies in our dedicated faculty, modern infrastructure, advanced laboratories, and strong collaborations with industries, research organizations, and government agencies. The institute takes pride in its
MSME-approved Business Incubator (HI/BI)
DSIR-SIRO recognized Research & Development Centre
, and the
StartinUP-supported Technology Business Incubator
, which has received a
₹2.5 Crore grant
from the Government of Uttar Pradesh to promote innovation and entrepreneurship. These initiatives have created a thriving startup ecosystem where students and faculties transform innovative ideas into impactful technologies and successful ventures.
Education at LIET is not merely about earning a degree—it is about developing the knowledge, ethics, skills, values, and confidence required to excel in a competitive global environment. Along with technical competence, we place equal emphasis on leadership, ethics, communication, teamwork, and lifelong learning.
At LIET, student success is driven by our strong placement ecosystem.
Through structured training in aptitude, technical skills, communication, personality development, coding, and interview preparation, we equip students with the competencies required by today’s industry. Our Corporate Resource Centre collaborates with leading companies to provide quality internships, career guidance, and placement opportunities, enabling our graduates to build successful and rewarding careers.
I invite aspiring students, parents, academicians, researchers, and industry partners to become a part of the LIET family. Together, let us build an environment where innovation flourishes, talent is nurtured, and future leaders are shaped.
“Success is not defined by the knowledge we acquire, but by the positive impact we create through that knowledge.”
