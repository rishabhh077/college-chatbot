About Us
Lloyd Institute of Engineering and Technology (LIET) celebrates a momentous achievement as it receives recognition as a Host Institute (HI/BI) from the Ministry of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSME) of India. This acknowledgment marks a significant milestone in LIET’s journey, affirming its commitment to fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in the engineering domain.
With this recognition, LIET is poised to provide a conducive environment for aspiring entrepreneurs and innovators, facilitating their growth and success. It brings immense joy and pride to the LIET community, as they continue to strive for excellence in education and contribute to the nation’s socio-economic development.
LTBIF: Empowering Innovation through MSME Support
The
Lloyd Technology and Business Incubator Foundation (LTBIF)
is a registered incubator under the
Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India
, dedicated to nurturing innovation-led entrepreneurship. As a recognized MSME incubator, LTBIF plays a pivotal role in transforming early-stage ideas into impactful ventures through structured incubation programs and government-backed support schemes.
Through its dynamic
Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell)
, students at LIET have actively participated in
MSME Idea Hackathon 2.0 and 3.0
, successfully transitioning their startup ideas from the ideation phase to
proof-of-concept (PoC)
development. These initiatives have led to multiple incubated startups receiving
financial assistance
from MSME, enabling them to build prototypes, file for intellectual property rights, and initiate early-stage commercialization.
LTBIF places strong emphasis on
inclusive entrepreneurship
, with focused initiatives to support
women founders and student innovators
. Dedicated mentorship, targeted sessions on MSME funding schemes, and business model refinement workshops help them gain the confidence and tools to build scalable ventures.
Additionally, LTBIF enhances startup visibility and ecosystem engagement by hosting
MSME Day events
startup exhibitions
, and
industry meetups
in collaboration with local business stakeholders. These events foster meaningful networking, showcase innovations, and create valuable
market linkages
for emerging entrepreneurs.
