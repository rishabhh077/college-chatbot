Things to Remember
In the wake of covid-19, many colleges in institutions have postponed or rescheduled the dates
of their exams. Many entrance exams have either been canceled or postponed until further notice.
To keep themselves ahead of any news related to engineering courses, the candidates must keep
checking the websites of the colleges time and again for any official notice to avoid any kind
of foresight. However, the tentative dates of these top10 upcoming exams are mentioned in the
calendar above for the convenience of students.
Students must also remember that all these entrance exams do not have the same eligibility
criteria, thus, they must ensure the exam they're appearing for suits their requirement and that
they satisfy the criteria required to attempt these exams entirely.
Eligibility Criteria for Engineering Entrance Exams
Eligibility criteria for entry into Btech courses intersect on these common grounds for most
colleges in the country. Despite the specifics being varied, students must make sure they
fulfill the eligibility criteria required to attempt these exams, and that they fully comply
with the guidelines mentioned on the official website of the college they’re applying into.
The students must have passed their 12th standard with subjects including physics,
chemistry, maths, and English in some cases.
The students must have secured 45% to 75% in their 12th to be eligible for these exams.
The specific percentage varies from college to college but falls under the range of
mentioned
percentages. There is a 5% relaxation in the minimum required percentage for students
belonging to SC ST and OBC category.
The students must have passed 12th from a recognized Board of Education.
