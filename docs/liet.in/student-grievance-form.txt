A Platform to Hear, Address, and Resolve Student Concerns.
Student Support & Resolution
The Student Grievance Form
provides a platform for students to raise concerns or complaints related to academic, administrative, or campus issues. It ensures that every grievance is heard and addressed in a fair and transparent manner. Students can submit their concerns confidentially through this form. The institution is committed to reviewing each grievance promptly and taking appropriate action. This process helps maintain a supportive and respectful learning environment. Students are encouraged to share their feedback to help improve institutional services and facilities.
