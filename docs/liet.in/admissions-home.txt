OVERVIEW
ADMISSION PROCESS: 2026–27
Eligibility
As per AICTE / AKTU norms.
Applicant should be a citizen of India.
ADMISSION TO MCA FIRST YEAR:
Admission through CUET Score:
85% seats of total approved intake will be filled through UP State Counselling and remaining 15% seats of approved intake will be filled under management quota.
Direct Admission:
Candidate must have passed Bachelor’s degree course of 03 Years minimum duration from any recognized Indian University; or its equivalent, recognized by A.I.U. securing minimum 50% (45% for SC/ST) marks in aggregate. Candidate must have passed Mathematics at 10+2 level or Graduation level.
ADMISSION PROCESS
ADMISSION TO B.TECH. FIRST YEAR:
A- Admissions through AKTU Counselling (JEE (main) score)
State Counselling 2026/JEE (main)/AKTU qualified candidates have to report in the college with allotment letter issued by AKTU in favor of Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology, College Code: 153 for complete the admission process till date as mentioned on the allotment letter.
Bring Original Documents for Verification at the time of reporting.
Carry two photo copy sets of the Documents (self-attested).
Rest of the procedures would be completed in the college only.
B- Direct Admissions
Please visit www.liet.in and click on Link Admission Enquiry form and get yourself registered with the Instructions mentioned in the page.
You would be informed by the authorities to attend the test / interview.
If qualified, you have to report to the college on the date given by the College authorities with the academic documents, passport size photo and Aadhar card.

SCHOLARSHIP
Scholarship Continuation Policy – LIET
At Lloyd College, we believe in rewarding consistent academic performance and dedication. Scholarship continuation is reviewed at the end of each academic year, and students who meet the required criteria will have their scholarships extended for the following year.
Eligibility Criteria for Continuation
To continue your scholarship, students must:
Score at least 58% marks in the academic year, with no back papers (aggregate of both semesters).
Maintain a minimum of 80% attendance throughout the year (aggregate of both semesters).
Exhibit exemplary conduct, with no misconduct or disciplinary issues.
Submit fees on time, adhering to the stipulated deadlines.
SC/ST – 30%
Resident of J&K – 15%
Resident of North East – 20%
Important Notes
Scholarships awarded based on National Level Entrance Tests are subject to revision after the declaration of results each year.
