Senior Director Message
The Lloyd Technology Business Incubator Foundation (LTBIF) is a leading incubation center dedicated to fostering innovation and entrepreneurship. Recognized as a Host Institute/Business Incubator (HI/BI) by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME) and approved by UP Startup, LTBIF reflects LIET’s commitment to high-quality incubation and entrepreneurial growth.
With cutting-edge infrastructure, technical expertise, funding support, and mentorship programs, LTBIF provides students and startups with the necessary resources to transform innovative ideas into successful ventures. Our incubation cell fosters industry collaborations and offers access to a strong entrepreneurial network, enabling startups to thrive in a competitive ecosystem.
At LIET, we bridge the gap between academia and industry, nurturing talent and fostering creativity. Through LTBIF, we empower entrepreneurs with essential skills, guidance, and opportunities to drive innovation and contribute to the nation’s economic growth. We welcome students, startups, and industry leaders to join this transformative journey.

Lloyd Technology and Business Incubator Foundation (LTBIF)
The Lloyd Technology and Business Incubator Foundation is a catalytic force propelling entrepreneurship and technological innovation. Rooted in a vision of fostering a thriving ecosystem for startups, it provides a fertile ground where ideas germinate and flourish. With a robust network of mentors, investors, and industry experts, it offers invaluable guidance and support to budding entrepreneurs, empowering them to navigate the complexities of business development. Through tailored programs and resources, it accelerates the growth trajectory of promising ventures, catalyzing their journey towards market success. Embracing diversity and creativity, it cultivates a culture of collaboration and experimentation, where risk-taking is celebrated and failure is viewed as a stepping stone to success. By bridging the gap between academia and industry, it stimulates economic growth and drives technological advancement, positioning itself as a cornerstone of innovation in the global landscape. The Lloyd Technology and Business Incubator Foundation is not just a platform; it’s a springboard for transformative ideas to reshape the future.

Incubation support
Physical Space
Offers dedicated office space and facilities where entrepreneurs can work, collaborate, and develop their business ideas in a professional setting.
Access to Funding
Assists startups in identifying and securing financial resources through various funding options, including grants, investments, and loans.
Mentorship
Connects startups with experienced mentors who provide strategic advice, industry insights, and guidance to help navigate challenges and make informed decisions.
Networking Opportunities
Facilitates connections with other entrepreneurs, industry professionals, and potential partners through events, workshops, and networking sessions to foster collaboration and growth.
Training and Workshop
These combined offerings are educational and experiential programs designed to support startups and entrepreneurs by enhancing their skills and knowledge through both theoretical and practical approaches.
Legal and Administrative Support
Provides assistance with legal matters, regulatory compliance, and administrative tasks to ensure that startups can focus on their core business activities.
Market Research and Validation
Helps startups conduct market research to understand customer needs, validate their business ideas, and refine their strategies based on data-driven insights.
Prototype Development
Supports the creation and refinement of prototypes, allowing startups to test and iterate on their products or services before full-scale production.
Business Development Services
Offers strategic guidance and practical support in areas such as marketing, sales, and operations to help startups build and grow their businesses effectively.
