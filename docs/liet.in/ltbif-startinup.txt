About Start in UP
The
Lloyd Technology and Business Incubator Foundation (LTBIF)
is a
recognized incubator under the UP Startup Policy
, established by the
Government of Uttar Pradesh
to promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and job creation across the state.
Housed within the
Lloyd Institute of Engineering and Technology (LIET), Greater Noida
, LTBIF is committed to empowering early-stage startups and student entrepreneurs through strategic mentoring, seed funding support, and infrastructure assistance.
With a sanctioned
grant of ₹2.5 crore
under the UP Startup program, LTBIF provides:
Pre-incubation and incubation support
Access to labs, co-working space, and prototype development facilities.
Seed funding facilitation and investor linkage
Specialized mentoring for
student, rural, and women-led startups
Training, bootcamps, and policy awareness programs
LTBIF works closely with
state agencies, industries, and academic institutions
to strengthen the entrepreneurial ecosystem and translate innovative ideas into viable, scalable ventures.
Key Achievements under UP Startup Policy
UPStartup Recognized Incubator:
LTBIF is a certified incubator under the UP Startup Policy, recognized by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. This prestigious recognition empowers LTBIF to extend structured support to startups through funding, infrastructure, and ecosystem integration.
₹2.5 Crore Grant Support:
Under the UP Startup scheme, LTBIF has been awarded a grant of ₹2.5 crore, aimed at strengthening incubation infrastructure, startup seed support, and entrepreneurial development programs across the region.
Seed Funding & Infrastructure Support:
Startups incubated under LTBIF are eligible to receive seed funding, prototype development assistance, and access to high-end lab facilities, enabling them to scale their innovations effectively.
Innovation & Incubation Programs:
Regularly conducts state-backed bootcamps, hackathons, and startup challenges, aligned with the UP Government’s vision of building a robust entrepreneurial ecosystem.
Inclusive Innovation Initiatives:
LTBIF prioritizes the inclusion of youth, women, and rural entrepreneurs by offering personalized mentorship, funding navigation, and exposure to state-level startup opportunities.
Policy Advocacy & Industry Engagement:
Works closely with UP government departments and industry partners to facilitate policy awareness, startup registrations, and inclusion in state procurement and pilot opportunities.
