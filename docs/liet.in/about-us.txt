About Campus
Welcome to Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology
Where innovation meets learning, shaping confident, skilled, and future-ready technology professionals.
LIET Campus
Established since 2004
Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology
is a premier technology institute in Greater Noida, Delhi NCR, backed by
23+ years of educational excellence
and a strong legacy of trust. The institute delivers
industry-aligned programs
that blend academic depth with practical learning through modern labs and real-world exposure. With a dedicated focus on
skill development and career readiness
, LIET prepares students for emerging technologies and evolving industry demands. Its strong
training and placement ecosystem
has enabled students to secure opportunities with leading recruiters. Guided by experienced faculty and a vibrant academic environment, LIET nurtures innovation, critical thinking, and confident professionals for the global technology landscape.
Our Principle
At Lloyd Institute of Engineering & Technology, our principle is to nurture knowledge, character, and competence through meaningful education. We blend strong academic foundations with practical skills, ethical values, and critical thinking. Guided by integrity and excellence, we prepare students to innovate responsibly and lead confidently in a changing world.
Our Philosophy
We focus on building careers, not just degrees. Through practical learning and industry exposure, students grow confident and job-ready.
Key Of Success
Industry-focused learning, practical skills, expert mentorship, innovation mindset, and strong placement-driven career support.
"Not a Degree, A Deployment-Ready Technology Career."
