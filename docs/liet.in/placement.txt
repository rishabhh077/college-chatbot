Campus Placements
Lloyd Institute of Engineering and Technology (LIET) offers a strong platform for students to launch successful careers through industry-focused education and career-oriented training. With a robust industry–academic framework, LIET prepares students with the technical skills, practical exposure, and professional confidence required by today’s employers.
Every year, leading national and multinational companies recruit talented graduates from LIET across engineering and technology domains. Through dedicated placement support, skill enhancement programs, and industry collaborations, LIET continues to deliver consistent placement outcomes and career opportunities.
