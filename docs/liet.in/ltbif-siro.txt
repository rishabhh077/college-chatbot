SIRO Recognized Research Incubator
The Lloyd Technology and Business Incubator Foundation (LTBIF) is a SIRO (Scientific and Industrial Research Organization) recognized entity under the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India. This prestigious recognition underscores LTBIF’s commitment to advancing scientific research, industrial innovation, and technology-led entrepreneurship.
As a SIRO-certified incubator, LTBIF is positioned to:
Undertake and support applied R&D activities
Facilitate prototype development and product innovation
Enable access to research grants, tax incentives, and government-funded schemes
Collaborate with academic institutions, R&D labs, and industry partners for high-impact innovation
Promote IP generation and technology transfer among startups and researchers
