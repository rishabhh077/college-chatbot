Student Mentoring Committee
A dedicated governance team driving responsible professionals and visionary leaders.
This committee of the college is responsible for keeping a constant track of the students’ performance at the department level. The heads of the department along with the class teachers allocate a group of students to a mentee (faculty) who keeps track of the academic performance of the student. Extreme cases are dealt in the presence of the parents; some are referred to the Counsellors by the committee to resolve the issue.
